Instructions
  • Menu visibility - Toggle menus off/on with the checkboxes.
  • Move menus - Click down on a menu and drag it.
  • Pause/play - Tap the space bar or use the play/pause button.
  • Jump to time - Drag on the scrollbar to jump to any part of the mission.
  • Playback speed - Choose a multiplier from the drop down box at the bottom.
  • Zoom in/out - Use the +/- buttons, mouse scroll wheel, or pinch/expand on your mousepad.
  • Change perspective - Hit the "👁️" button at the bottom to toggle the centering of the camera between (a) the orbit path (b) Earth (c) Irion. Click and drag on the screen to change the viewing angle.
